## Category-by-Category Recommendations

### Raspberry Pi 5

**Best:** eMAG.ro
- Price: €60-80
- Shipping: 1-2 days
- Payment: Cash on delivery available

**Alternative:** Dedicated RPi shops
- raspberry-pi.ro (if available)

---

### Motors (JGB37-520)

**Best:** AliExpress
- ASLONG store: €4-7 each
- Buy 4x for Phase 1, 2x for Phase 0

**Backup:** TME.eu
- May have similar planetary motors
- Higher price but faster

---

### Motor Drivers

**Quality:** Mouser/Digi-Key
- VNH5019: €12 each (single channel)
- Better thermal performance, reliable

**Budget:** AliExpress
- BTS7960: €5 each (dual channel)
- Works fine for prototyping

---

### 2020 Aluminum

**Only:** AliExpress
- Ships in 1m pieces
- €8-12 per meter
- Search "2020 extrusion V-slot 1m"

**Local Alternative:** Check if metal shops in Timisoara sell 20x20mm aluminum angle/square tube
- May require drilling (no T-slots)
- More expensive per meter but instant availability

---

### Wheels

**Phase 0 (Testing):** AliExpress
- "120mm rubber wheel robot"
- €6-10 each

**Phase 1 (Final):** Alibaba
- "12 inch agricultural wheel lug tread"
- €20-40 each
- MOQ may apply (contact seller for small quantity)

**Local Alternative:** Cart/wagon wheels from Dedeman
- 250-300mm pneumatic wheels
- €15-25 each
- May need adapter for motor shaft

---

### Battery (6S LiPo)

**Local:** RC hobby shops in Timisoara
- Search "magazin modelism Timisoara"
- Instant pickup, higher price

**AliExpress:**
- "6S LiPo 4000mAh" or "6S LiPo 5000mAh"
- €25-35
- 2-3 week shipping

**HobbyKing:**
- EU warehouse available
- Faster than AliExpress, better quality

### Fasteners (M5, M8)

**Best Local:** Dedeman or Arabesque
- Buy individual pieces or small packs
- M5 and M8 bolts, nuts, washers

## Payment & Logistics Notes

### Romania-Specific
- **eMAG:** Accepts card, cash on delivery (rambur)
- **AliExpress:** Most accept Romanian cards
- **EU Shops:** VAT included, no customs surprises
- **Customs:** AliExpress orders >€150 may incur VAT + duty

### Shipping to Timis County
- **eMAG:** Courier (FanCourier, DPD) - 1-2 days
- **Dedeman:** Pickup or local delivery
- **AliExpress:** Posta Romana or courier - 2-4 weeks
- **EU:** DHL, UPS, or local courier - 2-5 days

|--------------|---------------|-------|
